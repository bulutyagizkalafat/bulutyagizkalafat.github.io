Clean Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · App: Clean Master (com.northstar.temizlikekibi)

Türkçe

Clean Master is a single-player mobile game published by AstorDEV ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app processes, why, and the choices you have. The game does not require an account, and the game itself never asks you to enter your name, email address or phone number, and never accesses your contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Game progress stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, upgrades, collections, daily rewards, language and sound/vibration settings) is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show optional rewarded videos (only when you tap a reward button) and occasional interstitial ads at natural level ends. The SDK may automatically collect and share with Google your IP address (which can be used to estimate your approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent before ads are requested. When the option is available, you can reopen your privacy choices from the game's Settings.

From version 1.0.1 onward, the game asks for a date of birth to decide whether to start game-controlled advertising. The date is used in memory for this choice and is not saved. The game starts AdMob and requests ads only for users aged 18 or over; packaged SDK components may still initialize automatically before this check.

3. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Earlier versions (through 1.0.1, version code 11) include Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. Depending on the version and settings, these services may have processed automatic app events, gameplay events (such as level progress or ad rewards), device and app details (model, OS, app version and language), an app-instance identifier, approximate location inferred from an IP address, and crash reports (such as stack traces and device state). Existing data from an earlier version is not erased merely by updating the app.

Version 1.0.2 (version code 12), when distributed, is configured to deactivate Firebase Analytics collection and disable automatic Crashlytics collection. The game does not start its Firebase gameplay-event logger in that version. Firebase SDK components remain packaged and may initialize when the app starts; these settings are not proof that no data can be processed. Google Mobile Ads processes advertising data separately as described above.

4. No in-app purchases

The game currently offers no in-app purchases and does not connect to Google Play Billing. Everything in the game is earned through play.

6. Your choices

  • Rewarded videos only start when you tap them.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Privacy > Ads; wording varies by device).
  • You can reopen ad consent choices from the game's Settings where available.
  • You can delete local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.
  • For questions or requests to delete data associated with the app, email us with the game name (see Contact). We will review and respond using controls available to us and Google where applicable. Because the game has no account, we may not be able to identify or delete a particular record held by a provider.

7. Retention and security

Google Mobile Ads states that the data it collects is encrypted in transit (TLS). Data held by Google follows the provider’s applicable retention policies and settings, which can vary by service and by when the data was collected; we do not promise one fixed retention period for all provider records. Updating to version 1.0.2 does not delete data previously held by Google. Local game progress remains until you clear app storage or uninstall.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us; we will review the report and take appropriate action using controls available to us.

9. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and in other countries where it or its service providers operate, under its own safeguards.
