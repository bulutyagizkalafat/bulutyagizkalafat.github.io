Parking Boss Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026 · App: Parking Boss (com.northstar.otoparkoperatoru)

Türkçe

Parking Boss is a single-player mobile game published by AstorDEV ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app processes, why, and the choices you have. The game does not require an account, and the game itself never asks you to enter your name, email address or phone number, and never accesses your contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Game progress stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, valet coins, upgrades, collections, daily rewards, language and sound/vibration settings) is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show optional rewarded videos (only when you tap a reward button) and occasional interstitial ads at natural level ends. The SDK may automatically collect and share with Google your IP address (which can be used to estimate your approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent before ads are requested. When the option is available, you can reopen your privacy choices from the game's Settings.

3. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They may collect gameplay events (for example level started or completed, stars earned, rewarded video watched, daily reward claimed, language changed), an app-instance identifier, device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language), approximate location derived from your IP address, and crash reports (stack trace, the device state at the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation identifier).

We configure Analytics so that this data is not used for ad personalization or ads measurement (from the very first launch), and Analytics does not collect the advertising ID. We do not link this data to your name, email address or any account.

4. No in-app purchases

The game currently offers no in-app purchases and does not connect to Google Play Billing. Everything in the game is earned through play.

6. Your choices

  • Rewarded videos only start when you tap them.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Privacy > Ads; wording varies by device).
  • You can reopen ad consent choices from the game's Settings where available.
  • You can delete local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.
  • To request deletion of analytics or crash data related to your device, email us (see Contact) and name the game; we will handle the request with the tools Google provides.

7. Retention and security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit (TLS). Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in our Google Analytics settings; Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. Advertising data held by Google follows Google's own retention policies.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and in other countries where it or its service providers operate, under its own safeguards.
